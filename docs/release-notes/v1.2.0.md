Sysora 1.2 goes further in explaining your PC: a health score where every point is accounted for, "why now?" when
something rises, before / after comparisons, a timeline of everything that happened, a troubleshooting mode, a
large-file finder and reports you can keep or share. As before, everything stays on your PC: no account, no
telemetry, no network connection.

## Highlights

- **PC Health**: a score out of 100 for CPU, memory, storage, disk activity, GPU, temperatures, stability and recent
  anomalies, computed from 15-minute averages so a short spike barely counts. Each area shows the points it takes off
  and why. What cannot be measured on your PC is shown as "Not available" and left out of the score.
- **Why now?**: when CPU, memory, disk, GPU or network usage rises, Sysora finds when it started, from what level, the
  application that most likely contributed (and how much of the increase it accounts for), what happened at the same
  time and whether it happened before. Every statement is labeled *observed*, *inferred* or *unknown*.
- **Compare**: now vs 1 hour ago, now vs yesterday, today vs yesterday, before / during / after a game, a game vs the
  previous one, or any two moments.
- **Since yesterday**: what changed since yesterday (applications, startup programs, Windows, devices, disk space,
  activity and alerts), on the Dashboard and the Changes page.
- **Recurring problems**: problems that come back on several days, with when they tend to happen and the application
  most often involved.
- **Timeline**: applications started and closed, games, alerts, spikes, detected changes, sleep, network and devices in
  one list. Each entry opens Replay at that moment or a before / after comparison.
- **Troubleshooting mode**: record a problem for 2 to 30 minutes with detailed collection, then read what was abnormal,
  the applications involved and what to try. Collection returns to normal by itself.
- **Large files**: an on-demand, read-only scan of a drive or folder for the files that take the most space, grouped by
  type, extension or folder. Sysora never moves or deletes anything.
- **Reports**: export PC Health, Diagnosis, Why now, Replay, game recaps, App Impact, Alerts, Changes, comparisons, the
  Timeline, investigations and large-file scans as an HTML page (prints to PDF) or as JSON.
- **Monitoring intensity**: Minimal (gaming, battery), Balanced or Detailed, in Settings.
- **Sysora's own impact**: its CPU, memory, disk writes and collection rate, with a warning if it ever stays above its
  CPU budget.
- **Reorganized navigation**: Dashboard, PC Health and Timeline first, then *Investigate* and *Details*.

## Improvements

- Sysora opens about 30% faster and uses less CPU during its first minutes: the released build is now precompiled.

## Fixes

- When you update, a setting added by the new version now starts at its default value. It could be read as zero
  before (for example a CPU budget of 0, which means no limit).
- Demo mode (`--demo`) no longer changes your settings or the "Start with Windows" entry.

## Update from 1.1 or PulseDesk

Download and run the new installer: it updates Sysora (or PulseDesk) in place. Your settings, local history, alerts,
detected changes and game sessions are kept. Close Sysora first, or let the installer close it.

## Install

1. Download **`Sysora-1.2.0-setup-x64.exe`** (or `-arm64` for Windows on ARM) below.
2. Run it. By default Sysora is installed for all users in `Program Files` (administrator rights needed); the first
   screen also offers **Install for me only**, which needs no administrator rights.
3. Start Sysora from the Start menu.

The installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may show "Windows protected your PC". Click
**More info**, then **Run anyway**. You can check the download against `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

**Requirements**: Windows 10 version 1809 or later, or Windows 11 (x64 or ARM64). Nothing else to install: the .NET
and Windows App SDK runtimes are included.

## Notes

- Sysora only shows values Windows actually provides; anything else appears as "Not available" (for example FPS,
  temperatures without a driver that reports them, or network usage per application).
- Recurring problems need at least 2 days of history, and comparisons with your usual activity about 4 hours.
- The free space history used by Compare starts with this version.
